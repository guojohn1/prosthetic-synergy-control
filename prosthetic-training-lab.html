Step 1 · You are the patient

You have residual muscle signals above the missing limb. Choose the prosthesis you want fitted — each one is a different control problem, with its own joints, physics and success criteria. The little previews are live simulations, not pictures.

Every device below is a real simulated mechanism: joint limits, inertia, damping and passive stiffness are integrated at 20 Hz. Your muscle signals are simulated too — this is a control-architecture sandbox, not a medical device.
